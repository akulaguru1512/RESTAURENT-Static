FAQ

Useful answers.

Can I place a real order here?

No. Cart, checkout and tracking are local demonstrations only.

Can you check table availability?

No. Reservations are stored only in your browser and are not sent to restaurant staff.

How are allergens handled?

Menu items contain editable sample allergen fields, but this project cannot guarantee kitchen safety. A real deployment should integrate verified restaurant allergen data and staff workflows.

Do reviews come from verified diners?

No. Seed reviews are marked sample content and user-entered reviews are marked local demo reviews.

Where is my data stored?

Demo state is stored in localStorage for the current browser profile and is not synchronized across devices.

Does the project support takeaway and delivery?

Both flows are represented in checkout calculations, but no real fulfilment service is connected.
